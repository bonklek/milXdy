# Link Browser

Link Browser keeps you on X while you inspect a link. Enable it, then Alt-click a link in X or Twitter to open it in a docked reader panel instead of leaving the page.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Apps & Features**.
2. Enable **Link Browser**.
3. Optionally pin **Links** to the side rail for direct access.

## Use it

- **Alt-click** a regular `http` or `https` link to open it in the panel.
- Drag the panel header or resize its edges to place it where you want.
- Use **Refresh** to reload the embedded page.
- Use **Open in new tab** when you want the full browser page or when a site refuses to display in the panel.
- Normal clicks, Ctrl-clicks, Cmd-clicks, middle-clicks, and keyboard-opened links keep their native browser behavior.

## Privacy and compatibility

Link Browser points a sandboxed panel at the link you choose. milXdy does not fetch or process that page’s contents. It stores the panel placement and most recently opened link locally in your browser.

Some websites prevent embedded display. When that happens, use **Open in new tab**.
