## Release Inventory

- Complete code-level remediation for all 43 UX and feature reliability audit findings.
- Isolate app lifecycle, surface-delivery, and idle-task failures so one app cannot strand shared work.
- Make startup, network, storage, worker, speech, and local-file workflows cancelable and recoverable.

- Remove X Like-animation, mutation-rescan, and relational-selector hot paths.
- Improve rail overflow, compact ordering controls, Root Visual grouping, dark surfaces, reaction state, and Wiki read-aloud expansion.
- Preserve the Firefox/Waterfox, browser-specific updater, fullscreen DM media, and Tweet PNG fixes prepared after `0.2.2`.
