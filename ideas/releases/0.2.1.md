Labels: `reminet`, `chat`, `ux`.

### Expanded collection detection and behavior

Labels: `maxxer`, `performance`, `settings`.

Goal:

- Expand Maxxer behavior to reliably recognized collections and expose advanced handling controls.

Controls:

- Maxxed.
- Silver/lower tier.
- Neutral.
- Minimized.

Also include complete reversible non-Milady minimization, preserving users with RemiStats beetle stats.

### Direct Tweet PNG upload to RemiNet

Labels: `reminet`, `security`, `experimental`.

Goal:

- Extend the shipped local Tweet PNG exporter with explicit RemiNet upload/share.

Requirements:

- Confirm endpoint/API.
- Confirm auth behavior.
- Add preview/review before upload.
- Complete privacy review.
- Never upload automatically.
